Hyland x MCC

Welcome to the Hyland Tech Outreach classroom within the Midtown Collaboration Center! In this classroom, we abide by these non-negotiable community agreements:

Respect People Respect students and facilitators
Respect Space Respect the classroom and equipment
Respect Time Focus when it is time to focus
Include Everyone Leave no one out, share experiences
Engage Meaningfully Participate, collaborate, and pay attention
Learn & Grow Explore new ideas, adjust after mistakes, and keep improving
Take Responsibility Be accountable for actions and the consequences that follow, be in charge of yourself, set an example for others, lead
Spread Positivity Be kind, friendly, uplifting and encouraging
Be Resilient Do not be afraid to fail, ask questions, and try things even if it is hard
